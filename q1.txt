A prime number is an integer greater than 1 that has exactly two positive divisors: 1 and itself.

To check whether a number is prime, test whether any integer from 2 up to its square root divides it evenly. If one does, the number is not prime. If none does, it is prime.

Numbers less than 2, including 1, are not prime. The number 2 is prime.

For example, 13 is prime because no integer from 2 to its square root divides it evenly.
